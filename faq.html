Frequently asked questions

Power outlets

Outlets are 120 volts, the same as in the United States.

Alcohol hours

Alcohol may not be served or sold between midnight and 9 a.m.

Drinking age

The drinking age in Taniti is 18, and it is not strictly enforced.

Language

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. Very little English is spoken in rural areas, especially by older residents.

Hospital and clinics

There is one hospital and several clinics. The hospital has many multilingual employees.

Safety

Violent crime is very rare in Taniti. As tourism increases, there are more reports of pickpocketing and other petty crimes.

Holidays

Taniti has many national holidays, and many attractions and restaurants close on these days, so plan ahead.

Currency

The United States dollar is the main currency in Taniti. However, many businesses also accept euros and yen. Several banks exchange currency, and many businesses accept major credit cards.
