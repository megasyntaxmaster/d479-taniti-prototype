Getting here and around

Getting to Taniti

Arriving by air

Almost all visitors arrive in Taniti by air.

Learn more

Taniti's small airport handles small jets and propeller planes. The airport is currently being expanded so larger jets can land within the next few years.

Arriving by cruise ship

A small cruise ship docks in Yellow Leaf Bay.

Learn more

Taniti's small cruise ship docks for one night per week.

Getting around Taniti

Public buses

Public buses serve Taniti City.

Learn more

Taniti's public bus route runs from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day.

Private buses

Private buses serve the rest of the island outside of Taniti City limits.

Taxis

Taxis are available in Taniti City.

Rental cars

Rental cars are available from a local rental agency near the airport.

Bikes and helmets

Bikes and helmets are available to rent from several vendors, and helmets are required by law.

Walking

Taniti City is fairly flat and very walkable.

Learn more

Many tourists stay in the area around Merriton Landing, which is easy to explore on foot.
