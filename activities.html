Things to do

Sightseeing

Photo placeholder: Taniti City and beaches

Taniti City and beaches

Many tourists spend most of their time in Taniti City, which has native architecture and white, sandy beaches that encircle Yellow Leaf Bay.

Learn more

Taniti City is flat and very walkable.

Photo placeholder: Boat and bus tours

Boat and bus tours

Choose to take breathtaking boat or leisurely bus tours around the island.

Learn more

Public buses serve Taniti City while private buses serve the rest of the island.

Photo placeholder: Volcano and rainforest hikes

Volcano and rainforest hikes

Stay healthy by hiking to Taniti's active volcano and trekking through the lush, tropical rainforests.

Learn more

The volcano is in the island's mountainous interior.

Entertainment in Merriton Landing

Many of these activities are located in Merriton Landing, a quickly developing area on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay.

  • Museum
  • Fishing tours
  • Snorkeling
  • Zip-lining
  • Pubs and microbrewery
  • Dance club
  • Movie
  • Helicopter rides
  • Arcade
  • Art galleries
  • Bowling
  • Golf course (coming soon next year)

Businesses may close on national holidays. Visitors should plan accordingly.
